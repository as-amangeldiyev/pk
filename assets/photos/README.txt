📸 HOW TO ADD AMELIE'S PHOTOS:
==================================================
1. Put your square photos into this folder:
   assets/photos/photo1.jpg  -> Volleyball Ace Photo
   assets/photos/photo2.jpg  -> Ice-Cold Jäger Photo
   assets/photos/photo3.jpg  -> Admissions Crew Photo
   assets/photos/photo4.jpg  -> Golden Hour Candid Photo
   assets/photos/photo5.jpg  -> Match Point Victory Photo
   assets/photos/photo6.jpg  -> Legend Photo

2. Supported formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp
   (If you use .png, simply update the file extension in index.html, e.g. photo1.png)

3. Square 1:1 aspect ratio looks best, but rectangular photos will also automatically
   fit cleanly with rounded corners and polaroid borders!
